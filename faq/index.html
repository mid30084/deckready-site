FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about tagging your music with DeckReady. For the full workflow, see how DeckReady works.

How do I add Beatport tags to my MP3 files?

Open DeckReady, click Choose folder or files and then Analyze. The app looks up every track on Beatport and puts confident matches into Ready to Write. Check the tracks you want and click Write Beatport Tags.

Why is the genre, BPM or key wrong in my DJ software?

Your DJ software shows what it has read from the files, and many downloaded or promo files come with empty or inconsistent tags. DeckReady replaces them with Beatport’s data. After writing, reload the tags in your DJ software — until then it keeps showing the old values.

Does DeckReady support WAV, AIFF and FLAC?

Yes. DeckReady reads and writes MP3, FLAC, AIFF and WAV. WAV files get ID3 tags, and the basic fields — title, artist, album, genre, date and track number — are also written as RIFF INFO. WAV files larger than 4 GB (RF64) are not supported.

Is it safe for my files? Does it change anything before I confirm?

The analysis only looks for matches and does not change your files. Nothing is written until you click Write Beatport Tags, and the Which Tags to Write window lets you keep or remove each field first. Files removed by the duplicate finder go to the system Trash or Recycle Bin, so you can restore them.

Which tags are written?

Title, artist, remixer, album, genre, BPM, key, label, track number, release date, catalog number and artwork. For each of them you can take Beatport’s value, keep your own or remove the field.

Is the key written in Camelot notation?

No. DeckReady writes the key the way Beatport names it, in short form: Am, F#m or Eb.

What if the wrong version (Original, Extended, Remix) is matched?

DeckReady compares your file’s length with the versions on Beatport, and tracks it isn’t sure about go to Review Manually. Use Choose Version Manually to see every version of the track, listen to the previews and pick the right one. You can also add a version by pasting its Beatport link.

Will my cue points and ratings in the file be erased?

Only if you let them. Tags that Beatport doesn’t have — a rating, DJ software markers, links — are listed under Other tags in the files. Keep them checked and they stay in the file.

What if a track isn’t found?

It goes to No Matches. Most often the file has no tags and a file name that says little. Fix the name or tags and click Re-analyze, or find the track on Beatport and add it by link.

Does it work on Windows and Intel Macs?

Yes. DeckReady runs on macOS 12 or newer, on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs, and on Windows. There is a separate download for each.

Is DeckReady free?

Yes, DeckReady is a free download for macOS and Windows. Get it from the download section.

Do I need a Beatport account?

Yes. DeckReady signs in to Beatport with your Beatport account to look up tracks. You also sign in with Google once to use the app.

Can it find duplicate tracks?

Yes. Find Duplicates looks for copies among all analysed files and marks the best copy to keep — lossless before MP3, a higher bitrate before a lower one. The extras go to the system Trash or Recycle Bin, and one copy of every track always stays.

Why doesn’t rekordbox show the artwork of my WAV files?

rekordbox doesn’t show embedded covers for WAV files. This is a rekordbox limitation: the cover is written correctly and other software shows it.
