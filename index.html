How it works

Everything DeckReady does, one scroll at a time.

01 · Analyze

Point it at your folders.

DeckReady reads every MP3, FLAC, AIFF and WAV and sorts each track into a tab: ready to write, needs a look, or nothing to do. Tick the ones you want and write the tags in one click.

02 · Versions

The right version. Every time.

Extended, Original or Radio Edit? DeckReady compares the length of your file with every version on Beatport and picks the one you actually have.

03 · Review

Doubtful? You decide.

Close calls wait in Review Manually. Listen to your file and the Beatport preview, compare the waveforms and choose another version in one click.

04 · Tags

Every tag, your way.

Before writing you see all the tags in your files. Take each one from Beatport, keep your own, or remove junk like promo-site comments. Click the rows to try it.

05 · Duplicates

Duplicates, gone.

Find copies of the same track across all your folders, hear them side by side and send the extras to the Trash — never deleted for good.

06 · Formats

Every format you play.

MP3, FLAC, AIFF and WAV. DeckReady writes title, artist, album, genre, BPM, key, label, date, catalog number and artwork — in the right place for each format. WAV gets both ID3 and RIFF INFO, so every app can read it.

07 · DJ apps

Ready for your DJ software.

No more cryptic file names and empty columns. After DeckReady, rekordbox, Serato and Traktor show proper titles, artists, BPM, key, genre and artwork as soon as they re-read the tags.

08 · Platforms

Mac, Intel or Windows. Always up to date.

Native apps for Apple silicon, Intel Macs and Windows. New versions download and install themselves.
